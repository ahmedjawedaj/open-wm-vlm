# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to contribute, ask questions and discuss results. Be respectful, credit others' work, and critique ideas and evidence rather than people. Disagreement and negative results are part of research.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam and deliberate disclosure of private information are not accepted. Do not post credentials or another person's personal information in issues, pull requests or discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation to keep the project constructive. If a discussion becomes unsafe, contact the maintainer through an available private channel on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/ahmedjawedaj). Public reports should omit sensitive details. Reports will be handled with discretion, subject to the limits of the reporting channel.
